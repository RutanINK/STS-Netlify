File: Finished Good Component Part*.csv exported from your Databricks BOM dashboard.
How It Works
1
A supervisor adds a component (e.g. .75X4.5A) to the Shortages list as Out of Stock or Low Quantity.
2
STS looks up the BOM and finds every finished base SKU that uses that component — all color variants included.
3
When an area leader parses a Sabertooth schedule, any affected SKU shows:
⛔ Scheduling blocked — requires .75X4.5A — Out of Stock
4
The uploaded BOM is saved to Supabase and shared with all users automatically — no one else needs to re-upload it.
